Short Biography

My name is Akinwale Oladimeji, and I am a PhD student in Geography at the School of Earth, Environment and Sustainability, University of Iowa. My academic work focuses on spatial analysis and the use of geospatial data to understand patterns and processes across different environments. Through my research, I apply spatial thinking to real-world problems, with experience working on topics related to human–environment interactions.

Professionally, I am interested in geospatial intelligence (GEOINT), GIS, and data-driven decision-making. I am particularly focused on using geospatial tools such as ArcGIS, QGIS, Python, and web-based visualization platforms to analyze, interpret, and communicate spatial data. My goal is to apply these skills in areas such as intelligence analysis, urban systems, environmental monitoring, and policy support.

Personally, I enjoy learning new technical skills, building interactive maps, and exploring how effective visual design can improve the communication of complex geographic information. I am also interested in interdisciplinary work that connects geospatial technology with real-world applications across multiple sectors.
