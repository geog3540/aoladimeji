Short Biography

My name is Akinwale Oladimeji, and I am a graduate student with academic interests in geography, geovisualization, human-wildlife interactions, and spatial analysis. My work focuses on understanding how people, wildlife, and landscapes interact across different environments. Professionally, I am interested in GIS, cartography, data visualization, and using spatial tools to support research and decision-making. Personally, I enjoy learning new technical skills, creating maps, and exploring how visual design can make complex geographic patterns easier to understand.
